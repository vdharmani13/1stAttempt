What We Do

Our Core Practice Areas

We combine deep doctrinal expertise with practical courtroom experience to deliver effective, commercially-aware legal counsel across four specialised domains.

01
⚖️

Corporate Litigation

We represent corporates, promoters, and institutional clients in high-stakes commercial disputes before the High Courts, Supreme Court, Company Law Tribunals, and other specialised forums. Our work spans shareholder disputes, oppression & mismanagement, contractual claims, and regulatory proceedings.

Explore Practice →
02
🔏

Arbitration

We advise and represent clients in domestic and international commercial arbitrations under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, as well as ICC, SIAC, LCIA, and DIAC rules. Our team handles the full lifecycle — from drafting arbitration clauses to enforcement of awards.

Explore Practice →
03
🏛️

Insolvency & Bankruptcy

With extensive experience before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) and National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), we advise financial creditors, operational creditors, resolution applicants, and corporate debtors on all aspects of the IBC, 2016 — from initiation through resolution plan approval.

Explore Practice →
04
📊

Securitisation & Debt Recovery

We advise banks, NBFCs, asset reconstruction companies (ARCs), and borrowers on enforcement actions under the SARFAESI Act, 2002, proceedings before Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRT), and structuring of securitisation transactions and security enforcement strategies.

About the Firm

A Practice Built on Precision & Integrity

Dharmani Advocates is a boutique law firm with a focused practice in commercial and financial law. Founded on the principle that every client deserves rigorous, honest, and results-oriented legal counsel, we have built a reputation as trusted advisers to corporates, banks, financial institutions, and high-net-worth individuals.

  • Appeared before Supreme Court of India, various High Courts, NCLT, NCLAT, DRT and DRAT
  • Expert advisory on cross-border insolvency and international arbitration
  • Deep relationships with co-counsel across all major Indian cities
  • Committed to transparent, fixed-fee and outcome-linked engagements
