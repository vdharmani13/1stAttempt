Our Story

About Dharmani Advocates

A boutique law firm defined by expertise, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to our clients' interests — in every courtroom, in every tribunal, on every matter.

Who We Are

A Focused Firm for Complex Matters

Dharmani Advocates was founded with a clear purpose: to provide top-tier legal representation in the specialised areas of corporate litigation, arbitration, insolvency & bankruptcy, and securitisation — areas that demand not just legal knowledge, but deep familiarity with the procedural and commercial realities of Indian courts and tribunals.

Over the years, we have appeared in some of India's most significant commercial disputes and insolvency proceedings, representing financial creditors, promoters, resolution professionals, banks, and corporates. Our practice is built not on volume, but on the quality, depth, and seriousness we bring to every brief.

Our Philosophy

Principles That Guide Our Practice

Every decision we make — from the cases we accept to how we approach each hearing — is guided by a set of foundational principles.

⚖️   Integrity Above All

We give clients honest assessments of their matters, even when the news is unfavourable. Our reputation is built on candour, not false comfort. We never compromise on professional ethics.

🎯   Results-Oriented Strategy

Legal proceedings are means to an end. We craft strategies with the commercial outcome always in focus — conserving client resources while maximising the probability of a favourable resolution.

📚   Mastery of the Law

Our narrow focus means our team develops genuine depth. We track every significant judgment, every legislative amendment, and every regulatory development in our practice areas in real time.

🤝   Client Partnership

We treat clients as partners, not just instructions. Regular updates, transparent billing, and accessible senior counsel are not special offerings — they are the standard every client receives.

⚡   Agility & Responsiveness

Commercial disputes move fast. We maintain lean, agile teams that can mobilise quickly — filing urgent applications, securing interim relief, or responding to rapidly evolving situations without delay.

🌐   Cross-Jurisdictional Expertise

With deep networks of co-counsel across India and relationships with international arbitration practitioners, we can coordinate multi-jurisdictional strategies seamlessly.

Where We Practice

Courts & Tribunals

We maintain active practice before the following forums across India.

Supreme Court of India

Special Leave Petitions, Writ Petitions, appeals in commercial matters.

High Courts

Commercial courts, writ jurisdiction, company matters across multiple High Courts.

NCLT & NCLAT

Insolvency proceedings, company law disputes, oppression & mismanagement matters.

DRT & DRAT

Debt recovery, SARFAESI enforcement, securitisation challenges.

Arbitral Tribunals

Domestic and international arbitrations — ICC, SIAC, LCIA, DIAC, ad hoc.

Regulatory Forums

SEBI, RBI, Competition Commission (CCI), and other regulatory proceedings.
