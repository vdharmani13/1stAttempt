About Dharmani Advocates

A boutique law firm defined by expertise, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to our clients' interests — in every courtroom, in every tribunal, on every matter.

Who We Are

A Legacy of 47 Years. A Vision for the Future.

Dharmani Advocates is a specialised full-service law firm built on a rich legacy of over 47 years of experience in litigation. Well rooted in the ethos of this legacy, the firm has evolved into a modernised, cutting-edge practice with a global outlook — offering a diaspora of services that are transactional, regulatory, advisory, dispute resolution and tax related.

The firm operates from four major offices at Chandigarh, Chennai, Shimla and Una, and through associate offices across all districts of Punjab, Haryana and Himachal Pradesh. As we operate in the era of globalisation, the need for niche services and alternate redressal mechanisms such as arbitration has gained special focus — primarily in Arbitration, Banking & Finance, Insolvency, Due Diligence, Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets. The strength of the firm lies in its specialised services in these domains.

Our Ethos

The Principles That Define Us

We believe that change is the only constant. Our ethos reflects our commitment to continuous learning, rigorous analysis, and genuine client partnership.

📖   Learning and Evolving

We believe that change is the only 'constant' and hence we keenly focus on the latest developments in the field of law to learn, improvise and deliver the best to our clients.

🔍   Research and Analysis

We uphold a vision to exceed client expectations through insightful research and analysis to understand, interpret and resolve the issue at hand.

🤝   Tactical Teamwork

We derive our strength from the united efforts of our team that is diverse, well-balanced, experienced and proactive. The amalgamated efforts of each member form the basis of every accomplishment of the firm.

🎯   Realistic Resolution

We have a pragmatic approach towards dispute redressal by extending only realistic resolutions to our clients since we believe in building long-term business relationships in addition to handling the issue at hand.

⚙️   Meticulous Management

We ensure that cases entrusted to us are managed at the micro-level to eliminate information lapses. We provide regular daily updates on the status of proceedings through our litigation management system.

🌐   Global Outlook

As we operate in the era of globalisation, we combine traditional values of trust, integrity and hard work with a progressive, research-based approach to deliver seamless legal services to both domestic and international clients.

Where We Practice

Courts & Tribunals

We maintain active practice before the following forums across India.

Supreme Court of India

Special Leave Petitions, Writ Petitions, appeals in commercial matters.

High Courts

Commercial courts, writ jurisdiction, company matters across multiple High Courts.

NCLT & NCLAT

Insolvency proceedings, company law disputes, oppression & mismanagement matters.

DRT & DRAT

Debt recovery, SARFAESI enforcement, securitisation challenges.

Arbitral Tribunals

Domestic and international arbitrations — ICC, SIAC, LCIA, DIAC, ad hoc.

Regulatory Forums

SEBI, RBI, Competition Commission (CCI), and other regulatory proceedings.
