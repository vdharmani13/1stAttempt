Founding & Senior Partners

Founder Partner

Vipul Dharmani

Symbiosis Law School, Symbiosis International University, Pune  |  Bar Council of Punjab & Haryana
✉ vipul@dharmaniadvocates.com 📞 +91-97800-41313

Vipul is the Founding Partner at Dharmani Advocates and has played a pivotal role in the development of the firm. Academic excellence coupled with a passion for the legal profession led him to enrol as an Advocate with the Bar Council of Punjab and Haryana, where he has built a distinguished practice representing reputed companies before courts, tribunals, regulatory bodies and forums across India.

His practice areas span domestic and international commercial arbitration, banking and finance disputes, debt recovery and securitisation, civil-criminal, corporate, infrastructure and constitutional matters. His areas of expertise include arbitration, securitisation, asset reconstruction, consortium banking, domestic and external commercial borrowings and debt restructuring. He also advises clients on corporate restructuring, mergers and acquisitions, corporate governance, and all types of commercial and corporate contracts and joint ventures.

Vipul has especially proved his mettle in debt recovery and securitisation law by representing various reputed banks, financial institutions and asset reconstruction companies — both domestic and multinational — before Debt Recovery Tribunals, High Courts and other forums. His highly pragmatic, solution-oriented approach has helped the firm build long-lasting business relationships and establish itself as a one-stop solution for corporate clients.

Arbitration Securitisation Asset Reconstruction Banking & Finance Debt Recovery Corporate DRT / DRAT

Senior Partner

Varinder Dharmani

47+ Years of Practice  |  Certified Mediator
✉ varinder@dharmaniadvocates.com 📞 +91-98829-00512

Varinder Dharmani is a Senior Partner at the firm who heads the firm's business for the state of Himachal Pradesh, bringing over 47 years of practice. His major areas of expertise are criminal, civil and various kinds of consumer disputes. He regularly appears before various High Courts, Trial Courts, regulatory bodies and Tribunals.

Varinder has carved out a niche for himself in the field of criminal law and is a force to reckon with in Himachal Pradesh. He has also acted for renowned corporate houses in insurance, manufacturing, banking, healthcare, real estate and consumer goods. He brings rich experience in property related disputes — real estate, infrastructure projects, employment agreements and succession laws — and is on the legal advisory panel of various leading corporates and industrial houses.

His vast experience handling insurance claims against leading insurance companies over four decades has made him one of the most sought-after names for litigation in this field. Being a certified mediator is another feather in his already distinguished hat. On the steady foundation of his able guidance, the firm has managed to flourish across a diaspora of legal practice domains.

Criminal Law Civil Litigation Consumer Disputes Insurance Real Estate Mediation High Courts
