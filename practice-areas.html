Corporate Litigation

Corporate disputes are rarely simple. They involve intersecting layers of contract law, company law, securities regulation, and procedural complexity — often unfolding simultaneously across multiple forums. At Dharmani Advocates, corporate litigation is not a peripheral practice area; it is the core of what we do.

We represent promoters, corporations, institutional investors, and financial creditors in the full spectrum of commercial disputes — from contractual breaches and injunctions to complex multi-party shareholder disputes and regulatory enforcement actions. Our court-room experience spans the Supreme Court of India, various High Courts, the NCLT/NCLAT, and Commercial Courts.

Our Services Include

Shareholder & Boardroom Disputes Oppression and mismanagement petitions under the Companies Act, 2013 — protecting minority shareholders and challenging improper board actions.
Commercial Contractual Claims Breach of contract, specific performance, injunctions, and damages before High Courts and Commercial Courts.
Injunctions & Interim Relief Securing urgent interim orders — including attachment before judgment, injunctions restraining asset dissipation, and stay applications.
Company Law Proceedings Winding up petitions, reduction of capital, scheme of arrangement approvals, and other matters before the NCLT and High Courts.
Regulatory & Securities Disputes Representing clients before SEBI, SAT (Securities Appellate Tribunal), and in judicial review of regulatory orders.
White-Collar & Fraud Matters Advising corporates and individuals facing fraud investigations, Enforcement Directorate proceedings, and related civil claims.

Illustrative Experience

  • Successfully represented a promoter group in a landmark oppression & mismanagement petition before NCLT, securing reinstatement on the board.
  • Obtained urgent injunctive relief for a listed company restraining a hostile takeover attempt pending full adjudication.
  • Represented a major financial institution in recovery proceedings before the High Court involving disputed corporate guarantees exceeding ₹500 crore.
  • Advised a foreign institutional investor in cross-border enforcement of a commercial judgment across Indian jurisdictions.

Arbitration

Arbitration has become the preferred dispute resolution mechanism for sophisticated commercial parties — both in domestic transactions and cross-border deals. Dharmani Advocates brings the same rigor and preparation to arbitral proceedings as we do to courtroom litigation, with the added advantage of deep familiarity with the procedural rules and culture of major arbitral institutions.

We act as counsel in domestic arbitrations under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and in international commercial arbitrations under the rules of leading institutions including the ICC, SIAC, LCIA, and DIAC. We also advise on the drafting and negotiation of arbitration clauses, challenges to awards, and enforcement of foreign awards under the New York Convention.

Our Services Include

Domestic Commercial Arbitration Representation in ad hoc and institutional arbitrations under the A&C Act, 1996, covering construction, energy, joint venture, and financial disputes.
International Arbitration Acting as counsel in ICC, SIAC, LCIA, and DIAC arbitrations involving Indian parties and cross-border commercial disputes.
Arbitration Clause Drafting Advising on selection of seat, governing law, institutional vs. ad hoc structure, and drafting pathfinder clauses to avoid jurisdictional pitfalls.
Interim Measures & Emergency Relief Applications under Section 9 and Section 17 before courts and tribunals, and emergency arbitrator applications under institutional rules.
Challenge & Setting Aside of Awards Petitions under Section 34 challenging domestic awards, and opposition to such challenges. Also enforcement proceedings under Section 36.
Foreign Award Enforcement Enforcement of foreign arbitral awards in India under Part II of the A&C Act, and advising on enforcement of Indian awards abroad.

Illustrative Experience

  • Led counsel for a multinational in an ICC arbitration seated in Singapore, involving a ₹400 crore construction dispute with an Indian sub-contractor.
  • Successfully defended an Indian company in a SIAC arbitration against claims of breach of a joint venture agreement.
  • Obtained a favourable award in a domestic arbitration involving complex revenue-sharing disputes in the telecom sector.
  • Secured enforcement of a London-seated LCIA award against an Indian judgment-debtor under Part II of the A&C Act.

Insolvency & Bankruptcy

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC) has fundamentally transformed the commercial and financial landscape in India. Since its enactment, the IBC has been the subject of prolific litigation, and our firm has been at the forefront — advising and representing all categories of stakeholders in Corporate Insolvency Resolution Processes (CIRP), liquidation proceedings, and personal insolvency matters.

We appear extensively before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) — across its principal benches — and before the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) and the Supreme Court on IBC appeals. Our practice encompasses not just the procedural aspects of the CIRP but also the strategic advisory dimension: how to position a creditor's claim, how to evaluate a resolution plan, and how to navigate the intersection of the IBC with other legislation.

Our Services Include

Initiation of CIRP Filing applications under Section 7 (financial creditors), Section 9 (operational creditors), and Section 10 (corporate debtor) before the NCLT.
Creditor Representation Advising and representing financial creditors on the Committee of Creditors (CoC), claim verification, and resolution plan evaluation.
Resolution Applicant Advisory End-to-end advisory to resolution applicants — from due diligence through plan drafting, submission, and NCLT approval.
Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) / RP Support Legal advisory to IRPs and Resolution Professionals on their duties, powers, moratorium implications, and disputed transactions.
Avoidance Applications Filing and defending preferential transactions, undervalued transactions, and fraudulent trading applications under Sections 43–66 of the IBC.
Liquidation Proceedings Advising stakeholders in liquidation — proof of debt, distribution waterfall disputes, sale of assets, and dissolution proceedings.
Appeals & Judicial Review Appearing before NCLAT and the Supreme Court in appeals against NCLT orders, and seeking judicial review of CoC decisions.
Cross-Border Insolvency Advisory on cross-border insolvency matters, UNCITRAL Model Law applicability, and international coordination with foreign insolvency practitioners.

Illustrative Experience

  • Represented a leading PSU bank as financial creditor in a high-value CIRP involving a large infrastructure company, through resolution plan approval.
  • Advised the resolution applicant in the acquisition of a stressed steel manufacturing company through the IBC process.
  • Successfully challenged a resolution plan before NCLAT on grounds of procedural irregularity in CoC voting, leading to a fresh CIRP.
  • Represented an operational creditor in securing priority of claims and full payment under a resolution plan for a major FMCG company.

Securitisation & Debt Recovery

The SARFAESI Act, 2002 and the Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993 (RDBA) together constitute the primary legislative framework for secured creditor enforcement and debt recovery in India. Dharmani Advocates has extensive experience advising both lenders and borrowers in proceedings under these statutes, and in the structuring of securitisation and asset reconstruction transactions.

We represent banks, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), asset reconstruction companies (ARCs), and borrowers before Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRT) and Debt Recovery Appellate Tribunals (DRAT) across India, as well as in High Court and Supreme Court proceedings arising out of these matters.

Our Services Include

SARFAESI Enforcement Advising and assisting secured creditors in all stages of SARFAESI enforcement — demand notices, symbolic possession, physical possession, and auction proceedings.
DRT Litigation Filing and defending Original Applications (OA) and Securitisation Applications (SA) before DRT; Miscellaneous Applications for interim relief.
DRAT Appeals Appellate proceedings before DRAT against DRT orders, including deposit waiver applications and urgent stay petitions.
NPA Recovery Strategy Structuring multi-pronged recovery strategies — combining SARFAESI enforcement, DRT proceedings, and IBC mechanisms for maximum creditor recovery.
Securitisation Transactions Advisory on asset securitisation structures, assignment of receivables, security trustee arrangements, and regulatory compliance for ARCs.
Borrower Defence & Settlement Representing borrowers in challenging erroneous NPA classifications, wrongful SARFAESI action, and negotiating one-time settlements with lenders.

Illustrative Experience

  • Acted for a nationalised bank in a contested SARFAESI enforcement involving multiple secured assets across three states, achieving full recovery.
  • Represented an ARC in structuring and enforcing security interests acquired pursuant to a portfolio acquisition from a private sector bank.
  • Successfully challenged a wrongful NPA classification by a bank, obtaining an injunction and ultimately a settlement favourable to our borrower client.
  • Obtained deposit waiver before DRAT for a small-scale industry borrower challenging possession proceedings under Section 17 of the SARFAESI Act.
